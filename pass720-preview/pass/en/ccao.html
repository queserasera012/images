4

Evaluating output (21% · top priority)

A very confident answer
○ PickTreat it as a draft; a human checks the high-impact parts
× AvoidUse it as is
A precise statistic with no source
○ PickSuspect fabrication. Check the original or an authoritative source
× AvoidIt sounds plausible, so keep it
Citations from Research
○ PickRead what the source says
× AvoidThere is a link, so it is right
Numbers that are slightly off
○ PickRecalculate with code execution
× AvoidAsk again
Asked for an exact future number
○ PickTurn it into a range or model with stated assumptions (expectation mismatch)
× AvoidMake it give one number
5

Products and models

Choose a feature by what it is for

ProjectsInstructions + knowledge. The setup for repeated work. Shared with a team
ResearchSearches widely and reports with sources
ArtifactsA standalone piece of work you keep refining and share
MemoryRemembers across conversations
SkillsA fixed procedure, reused
ConnectorsDrive, Gmail, Slack and others. Data from the connected service is passed in

Models

HaikuFast and cheap: classify, extract, high volume
SonnetBalanced: the default
OpusMost capable: complex, important work
When to make a Project: at least 2 of repeated work / same background / same output format. Never mix two clients’ confidential material → one Project per company (Memory is per Project).

Governance

Four thresholds that decide if human review is required

StakesHow big the impact is
ReversibilityWhether it can be undone
AudienceWho sees it (external)
Regulatory exposureWhether regulation applies
You want to paste in confidential or personal data
○ PickCheck policy before it goes in. Minimum needed, anonymized
× AvoidIt is handy, so paste it
You want to analyze regulated data
○ PickCheck for an approved channel. If there is none, stop and ask the admin
× AvoidTry it on a personal account
Someone recommends a Skill of unknown origin
○ PickSend it to your organization’s review
× AvoidEnable it on your own judgment
A use the policy does not mention
○ PickAsk first
× AvoidIt is not banned, so it is fine
External deliverables or actions that can’t be undone
○ PickHuman review and approval
× AvoidSend the AI output as is
