Privacy policy

  • We do not collect any data. The app has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Your progress is saved on your device. If iCloud is on, it is also synced through your own iCloud account. We cannot see it.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple. We do not receive your payment details.
  • The app does not connect to any server run by us.
