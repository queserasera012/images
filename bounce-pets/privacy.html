Support and privacy policy. Last updated: October 2026.

Support

Questions, bugs, or a purchase that did not arrive? Contact us on X: @queserasera_012

To get your purchases (Premium Pack, Second Pet Slot, Advanced Upgrades) back on a new device, open the Shop and tap Restore purchases.

Privacy policy

  • We do not collect any data. The app has no account and no analytics. The developer never receives your progress or any personal data.
  • Your progress is saved on your device. If iCloud is on, it is also synced through your own iCloud account. We cannot see it.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple. We do not receive your payment details.
  • The app does not connect to any server run by us.
  • Rewarded ads (from version 1.1.0). Ads are shown only when you tap an ad button (double coins after a clear, continue after a game over, a free egg once a day; up to 5 per day). There are no ads that play on their own and no ads during play. Ads are provided by Google AdMob and limited to content rated up to PG. The first time you tap an ad button, the app asks once for permission to track (App Tracking Transparency). If you allow it, Google may use your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) to show personalized ads; if you don't, only non-personalized ads are shown. Google may collect the IP address and approximate location, ad interaction data and diagnostics to deliver ads. Your game progress is not sent to ads. See how Google uses data.
  • App updates (from version 1.0.1). To deliver small fixes, on launch the app asks Expo's update service (EAS Update) whether a newer version is available, and downloads it if so. It sends only the app version, the platform (iOS), and a random number the app creates for updates. This number is not linked to any device identifier, Apple ID or name. Your progress is not sent. Without a connection, nothing is sent and the game works as usual.
